# The Editorial Playbook — validated, per-topic content strategy

**Question it answers:** for each of our 9 segments, the most *engaging* and highest-*retention* way to present aggregated content, proven by real examples, with an honest medium-transfer judgment.

**Headline conclusion (high confidence):** Across every vertical and every study, the winning recipe is the **same backbone** — a *consistent, skimmable structure* + a *distinct human voice* + *genuine curation ("why it matters")* + *one deeper "big thing"* — differentiated per topic by **one signature recurring element**. The flashy formats you were drawn to (staged debate / "agent vs agent", sarcasm-as-structure, consensus-meters, gamified widgets) are **mostly contradicted by the evidence** for a text/email medium — but each has a *proven, text-native cousin* we keep. This is the key finding, and it revises the earlier direction.

> Evidence log with citations: [FINDINGS.md](./FINDINGS.md). Confidence tags are the researchers'; most magnitudes are vendor/creator-sourced (directional), and open rates are inflated ~15–20 pts by Apple Mail Privacy Protection — so we weight **clicks, replies, and 12-month retention** over opens.

## 1. The universal backbone (works in ALL 9 — high confidence)

1. **Consistent structure + same send time** → habit. Morning Brew treats a subscriber's *first-two-week* open rate as the top predictor of long-term retention. Habit, not novelty, is the #1 retention lever. *(Morning Brew, SparkLoop; 1440 >50% retention)*
2. **Smart Brevity per item:** bold one-line lead → **"Why it matters"** → tight bullets. ~40% shorter, scannable, mobile-first. *(Axios; used by every 1M+ brief)*
3. **Curation with a point of view** — "I read 200 things so you read 6." Commentary is *why people subscribe*; a bare link-dump is a top churn cause. *(TLDR ~40% open @1.6M; HubSpot)*
4. **One "big thing" per issue** — a deeper lead/explainer. This is where POV lives; "no point of view → nobody reads after month one." *(Pragmatic Engineer, Stratechery paid retention)*
5. **A distinct human VOICE** — the single hardest thing to copy, so the durable moat for an *aggregator* whose raw material is commodity. Drives both differentiation (growth) and retention. *(Morning Brew 42%@4M; The Neuron 0→700K on a human "cat" voice vs "robot-logo AI newsletters")*
6. **One signature recurring element** — a named ritual that creates appointment/anticipation. *(Morning Brew games credited as a retention driver)*
7. **Honest sourcing / trust** — deliver exactly what the subject promised; flag single-sourced items. Clickbait lifts opens once then accelerates churn. *(1440 trust positioning; Tangle)*
8. **One low-friction interaction = a link-based poll or a reply prompt** — replies are the strongest deliverability + engagement signal. NOT embedded widgets (see §3).
9. **Engagement levers:** recognizable human **sender name** (top open factor), concrete non-clickbait **subject** (the literal word "newsletter" cuts opens ~23.8%), optimized **preheader**, a **first-line hook**, ruthless mobile skimmability.

## 2. The contested frontier — voice vs novelty (this is the important nuance)

- **Settled:** consistency + curation + voice win. Not in dispute.
- **Genuine tension:** how much *novelty/cleverness* helps. **Red-team verdict (well-supported):** novel formats produce a **one-time spike that decays** ("novelty is non-renewable"); in head-to-head A/B tests the **simpler** email wins on clicks (plain text out-clicked a GIF hybrid by 42%; plain beat HTML ~17–21% on CTOR); and **the category leaders win with plain skimmable roundups + voice, not gimmicks.**
- **Resolution:** a signature element only helps if it's a **permanent recurring ritual** (renewable habit), never a rotating novelty. Voice is the safe differentiator; structure-gimmicks are the risky one.

## 3. Medium-transfer verdicts — your #4 question, answered with evidence

| Format loved elsewhere | Transfers to text/email? | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Curiosity-gap hook (YouTube/social) | ✅ as **subject + first line only** | Mechanism transfers; punish undelivered gaps harder (1-tap unsubscribe) |
| "One idea per piece" (TikTok) | ✅ | Scanning mirrors scrolling |
| Personality/voice (podcasts/creators) | ✅ **as prose** | The one thing that ports well; deliver in *writing*, not audio |
| **Debate / "agent vs agent" (podcasts)** | ❌ | Engine is *auditory* (rhythm, interruption). In text it **doubles length**, adds friction, and risks **false balance** (bothsidesism) |
| **Sarcasm / ironic tone (social/video)** | ❌ as structure | Sarcasm is **reliably misread in text** (no intonation); use dry wit as *garnish*, not the format |
| **Consensus-meter / animated data widget** | ❌ | Interactivity/animation **doesn't render** (AMP only in Gmail); becomes an image that vanishes with images-off / screen readers |
| **Gamified quiz / spin-to-win** | ❌ (widget) / ✅ (link-out) | Embedded games break in Apple Mail/Outlook; only a **static puzzle or link-out** works |
| Reaction GIFs / memes | ⚠️ sparingly | NN/g: **31% find animated emails annoying** (14% static); bloats size, trips spam |
| Embedded/autoplay video | ❌ | Most clients can't play it; use a static thumbnail linking out |
| Chart / data-viz | ⚠️ | Only as **static image + text takeaway + alt text** (images blocked by default, break in dark mode) |
| Interactive poll | ✅ **as click-tracked link** | This is the right way to "port interactivity" |

**Genuinely-proven "unique" formats we KEEP** (text-native, with hard evidence): multi-perspective **"both sides + our take"** (Tangle: ~90% annual retention, 99% monthly renewal, <1% unsub, 16% free→paid — the best documented retention in news), fact/opinion separation (Semaform — structure sound, retention unproven), **"Spot the Bug"** for dev (interaction is *cognitive*, and code IS text), the **deadpan headline stack** for satire, the **deconstructed playbook** for indie, the **single-stock thesis + transparent scoreboard** for small-cap.

## 4. Per-segment playbook

Every segment uses the §1 backbone. Below is the **persona**, the **signature element** (the proven differentiator to add), **voice**, top **proven examples**, and topic-specific **avoid**.

### 🤖 Tech / AI — *busy devs/founders, allergic to AI-slop*
- **Signature:** a **"So what / apply this"** line on each item (a prompt to try, repo to clone, "skip / worth 10 min") + **one rotating deep-dive**. 
- **Voice:** human, opinionated, lightly witty — visibly *not* a robot. **Proven:** The Neuron (0→700K on human voice), TLDR (~40%@1.6M), The Rundown ("how to apply" differentiator), Pragmatic Engineer (paid depth). 
- **Avoid:** AI-generated volume, clickbait (devs distrust it), embedded video.

### 📰 Mainstream News — *fatigued readers, want caught-up in 5 min, no doom*
- **Signature (flagship differentiator):** **One-Big-Thing dual-mode** (skim rail + one deep story) **+ multi-perspective "both sides + our take"** **+ a constructive close** ("one good thing"). This is where our cross-source Consensus idea belongs — but as **clean labeled text**, not an animated meter. 
- **Voice:** warm, named editor. **Proven:** **Tangle** (90% annual retention, <1% unsub), Guardian First Edition, NYT The Morning (5.5M), Axios AM, Reuters 2025 (40% avoid news → constructive close is retention insurance). 
- **Avoid:** the maximalist "everything" digest, outrage/partisan framing, clickbait.

### 💰 Business / Finance — *professionals & investors, want context fast*
- **Signature:** **"Why it matters + 3–5-year horizon"** framing on the lead + a recurring **static chart-of-the-day**. 
- **Voice:** smart-friend wit (Brew/Hustle) or calm-analytical (Daily Upside) by sub-audience. **Proven:** Morning Brew (42%, $75M), The Daily Upside (1M, ~45%, credits horizon-framing as anti-churn), **The Diff** (raised price $15→$20 and subs paid — hard willingness-to-pay signal), Matt Levine. 
- **Avoid:** charts with no text takeaway; one giant column as the *whole* product.

### ⚖️ Legal / Law — *attorneys, skeptical, signal-over-noise*
- **Signature:** **One-Big-Thing plain-English explainer** (what it holds / why it matters / what to watch) + **practice-area segmentation + preference center**. 
- **Voice:** authoritative plain-English; dry wit only as garnish. **Proven:** SCOTUSblog "Plain English", Lawfare analysis, National Law Review (25+ segmented topics), Above the Law (voice). 
- **Avoid:** legalese, memes/GIFs (read as unserious to senior legal), clickbait (lawyers punish it hardest).

### 👔 HR / People Ops — *HR leaders, want to look smart to leadership*
- **Signature:** per-item **"Why it matters (for HR)"** + one **narrative lead** + a **"Number of the week"** + a **one-tap peer-benchmark poll** ("how's your team handling RTO?"). 
- **Voice:** conversational, lightly witty (HR Brew = Brew voice for HR). **Proven:** HR Brew, Axios, WSJ "Project Habit" (recurring-feature reading predicts renewal). 
- **Avoid:** corporate jargon, internal-newsletter tropes (birthday shoutouts), self-promo.

### 💻 Developer / GitHub — *working devs, bullshit-allergic, Apple-Mail-heavy*
- **Signature:** opinionated curated links + read-time **+ "Spot the Bug"** (the rare interactive that works — cognitive, and code is text) **+ "Tool of the week" honest review** (incl. a caveat). 
- **Voice:** terse, funny, expert. **Proven:** TLDR, **Bytes** (~45% open + "Spot the Bug" signature), Console (criteria-based reviews), Pragmatic Engineer. 
- **Avoid:** link dumps, clickbait, cliffhangers (devs want complete value *this* issue), meme-heavy layouts.

### 🚀 Indie Hacker — *solo founders, read to steal something usable*
- **Signature:** the **deconstructed playbook** (one teardown: why it matters → the numbers → steal-able takeaways → opportunities) + **build-in-public transparent numbers incl. losses**. 
- **Voice:** analyst-operator peer, no hype. **Proven:** **Trends.vc** (50K subs / 1,000+ Pro on a fixed template), **The Bootstrapped Founder** (near-zero churn ~5/2,500/wk *because* it shares losses), Starter Story. 
- **Avoid:** vanity metrics/spin, self-promo, generic AI voice.

### 🎭 Satire / Absurdist — *readers who open for a laugh*
- **Signature:** the **deadpan headline stack** (the joke IS the headline — the one comedy format that *gains* in an inbox) + a **voice-led curated roundup** + one **fake-format set piece**. 
- **Voice:** straight-faced wire-service **deadpan** (NOT sarcasm — sarcasm misreads in text) + a strong narrator for curation. **Proven:** The Onion (headline-as-subject-line), Today in Tabs (voice is the product), McSweeney's (fake-format). 
- **Avoid:** explaining the joke ("😂/lol"), every-line-a-gag fatigue, forced humor on slow days, image-dependent punchlines.

### 📈 Micro / Small-Cap — *retail speculators, FOMO-prone but scam-burned*
- **Signature:** **single-stock deep-dive thesis with explicit kill-criteria/risks** + a **transparent scoreboard (wins AND losses)** + a "what moved & why" digest. The bear case + track record are the anti-scam trust signals. 
- **Voice:** analyst-guide who names the bear case. **Proven:** Stansberry/Tilson (deep-dive + interstitial updates), MicroCapClub, Hulbert Ratings (scoreboard auditing), Daily Upside. 
- **Avoid (compliance-critical):** hype/FOMO/"next 1000% runner"/countdown urgency (SEC/FINRA pump-and-dump red flags), reprinting paid IR (RedChip) as if it were independent research.

## 5. Contradictions & what could NOT be verified
- **Open rates are unreliable** (Apple MPP inflates ~15–20 pts; ~46% of clients). Optimize clicks/replies/retention.
- **Most magnitudes are vendor/creator-sourced** ("371% single-CTA", "40% shorter", "50%+ opens") — directional, not controlled. **Tangle's retention numbers** are the strongest hard evidence in the set.
- **Unproven at the numbers level:** Semaform and Ground News's Blindspot have no public retention data (structure is sound, effect assumed). Gamification lift figures are vendor one-off campaigns.
- **Genuine disagreement:** "distinct format" (differentiation/growth) vs "plain consistency" (retention). Reconciled as: *voice = safe differentiator; recurring ritual = safe novelty; interactive/animated gimmicks = net-negative in email.*
- **Tooling gap:** no Exa/Tavily/Firecrawl/academic MCPs — deeper access (peer-reviewed attention studies, first-party ESP retention datasets) would firm up the magnitudes.

## 6. Implications for Step 3 (generation)
- **Default treatment = the §1 backbone**, applied per segment, with that segment's **one signature element**. Make voice + structure the product.
- **Do NOT build** the animated consensus-meter, staged agent-vs-agent debate, or sarcasm-as-structure — the evidence says they underperform in email. Keep their proven text cousins (Tangle-style labeled multi-perspective; dry wit; static "who-covered-it" table; link-based poll).
- **Pilot order:** Tech first (backbone + "so what" + rotating deep-dive), then stand up the **News multi-perspective** format as the flagship differentiator (best retention evidence).
