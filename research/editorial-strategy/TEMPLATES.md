# Per-Topic Templates (Step-3 generation spec)

Every issue shares the **backbone**: concrete non-clickbait subject (never the word "newsletter") + preview; masthead (name · date · read-time · "N sources → 1"); a 1-line human hook; a **recurring ritual**; a **one-tap poll + reply prompt**; a human sign-off. Track clicks/replies/retention, not opens. No staged debate, no sarcasm-as-structure, no animated widgets/embedded video.

Each topic differs by its **opening engine** (the reader's job), its **signature section**, and its **voice**.

| Topic | Opening engine | Signature section | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tech/AI | Your problem | "If you're dealing with… → the fix" + Apply-this | human, anti-slop, lightly witty |
| News | Orientation | Both-sides + our take + good-news close | warm named editor |
| Business/Finance | Decision | Why-it-matters + 3–5yr horizon + chart | smart-friend / calm-analyst |
| Legal | Relevance | Plain-English explainer + practice-area segments | authoritative, dry-wit garnish |
| HR | Look-smart signal | Number-of-the-week + peer poll | conversational, lightly witty |
| Dev/GitHub | Utility | Tool-of-week review + Spot-the-Bug | terse, funny, expert |
| Indie | Steal-something | The Teardown (numbers→takeaways→opportunities) | analyst-peer, no hype |
| Satire | A laugh | Deadpan headline stack + set piece | straight-faced deadpan |
| Small-cap | Edge + trust | Thesis w/ kill-criteria + transparent scoreboard | analyst-guide, names the bear case |

---

## 🤖 TECH / AI — problem-first hybrid
1. 1-line hook
2. **🔧 IF YOU'RE DEALING WITH…** — 2–3 real pains → the release that helps → "Do this"
3. **🎯 The Big One (you'll get asked about)** — what happened / why it matters / the catch / how to read it
4. **🔗 Context** — raises, chips, moves (not to-dos)
5. **⚠️ Single-sourced** — flagged, not amplified
6. **🧪 One thing to try this week** (ritual)
7. Poll + reply + sign-off

## 📰 MAINSTREAM NEWS — orientation-first, calm
1. Editor's note (1–2 lines, named)
2. **5 big stories, one line each** (skim rail)
3. **🎯 The one to understand** — what happened / why it matters / what's next
4. **🔀 Both sides + our take** — facts → strongest case each way → our labeled take
5. **🌍 Also happening** — quick hits
6. **☀️ One good thing** (constructive close — retention insurance)
7. Poll + sign-off

## 💰 BUSINESS / FINANCE — decision-first
1. **📈 Markets snapshot** — index levels + one-line why (ritual open)
2. **🎯 The big move** — what happened / why it matters / 3–5-year angle
3. **The rundown** — 3–5 items, each with a "why it matters"
4. **📊 Chart of the day** — static image + one-line takeaway
5. Quick hits
6. Poll + sign-off

## ⚖️ LEGAL — relevance-first, segmented
1. **The 3 things that matter today** (top TL;DR)
2. **🎯 Plain-English explainer** — the big ruling: what it holds / why it matters / what to watch
3. **By practice area** — Litigation · Regulatory · BigLaw · Legal-tech (reader-selected)
4. Quick hits
5. **What to watch next**
6. Sign-off + preference-center link

## 👔 HR / PEOPLE OPS — signal + look-smart
1. **🎯 One narrative lead** — a workplace story with a "so what for people teams"
2. **Why it matters (for HR)** — 3–5 items, each with the HR implication
3. **📊 Number of the week** — one stat + so-what
4. **🗳️ Peer poll** — "how's your team handling X?" (results next issue)
5. Quick reads
6. Sign-off

## 💻 DEVELOPER / GITHUB — utility-first
1. **Ship list** — opinionated links + read-time + why-you-care
2. **🔧 Tool of the week** — what it is / what we like / one caveat / who it's for
3. **🐛 Spot the Bug** (recurring interactive — answer at the bottom)
4. **🧠 One deep explainer** — "why this matters to your stack"
5. Quick bits
6. Sign-off

## 🚀 INDIE HACKER — steal-something-first
1. **🎯 The Teardown** — one company/trend: why it matters → the numbers → steal-able takeaways → opportunities
2. **Build-in-public numbers** — real MRR / wins AND losses from the community
3. **Playbook bits** — tactics you can copy
4. **Reply prompt** — "what's your one blocker / your MRR?"
5. Sign-off

## 🎭 SATIRE — laugh-first (short)
1. **Deadpan headline stack** — 6–10 finished-joke headlines → links (the joke IS the payload; no "why it matters")
2. **🎭 The set piece** — one fake-format feature (memo / FAQ / listicle)
3. **Recurring bit** — e.g. "Bee vs Beaverton" or reader caption
4. Sign-off (deadpan)

## 📈 MICRO / SMALL-CAP — edge + trust-first
1. **⚠️ Standing line** — not advice; we flag paid IR (e.g. RedChip) as promotion
2. **🎯 The thesis** — one stock: hook → why now → business & numbers → valuation → **what would kill it**
3. **📊 Scoreboard** — open picks: entry / status / result (wins AND losses)
4. **What moved & why** — short digest
5. Sign-off
