## 🎯 THE BIG ONE — OpenAI says 10,000 agents cracked a $1M math problem. Then the knives came out.

**What all three sources agree on:** OpenAI claims an unreleased model — described as "significantly more capable than GPT-6 Astra" — produced a proof for a version of the **Navier–Stokes equations**, one of the seven Millennium Prize Problems and among the hardest open problems in math. It took roughly **88 hours**. *[TLDR, Superhuman AI, The Neuron — all 3]*

**How they say it was done** (only The Neuron carried the mechanics): OpenAI pointed **~10,000 agents** at the problem at once, with tools, code execution, and the ability to share discoveries — generating **2.7M agent messages and ~130B tokens**. The agents found a candidate proof (a fluid developing a singularity in finite time); **GPT-6 Astra then formalized and verified it in Lean**, a proof-checker. TLDR adds the wild implication OpenAI is leaning on: the proof describes conditions under which the equations — and "the laws of physics" — could theoretically break down.

**⚔️ The fight** *(this is why the story matters more than the math):*
- **The accusation** — NYU's **Tristan Buckmaster** and Anthropic's **Levent Alpöge** say they spent ~a year on a *similar* problem using an unusually rare approach. Buckmaster claims they'd uploaded drafts into **Codex**, and that OpenAI — which had access to those logs — then produced a proof using **the same unusual approach**. *[Superhuman AI, The Neuron]*
- **The denial** — OpenAI's **Sébastien Bubeck** calls the allegations "false and inflammatory" and says the proof was reached **independently**; **Sam Altman** has backed him. OpenAI says it merely "heard rumors" of breakthroughs and denies accessing the researchers' work. *[Superhuman AI, The Neuron]*

**Why it matters:** Beyond the trophy, this is becoming **Exhibit A** for a fear academics have voiced for years — that big labs quietly absorb ideas from the private work users feed their tools. And, as The Neuron argues, it reframes how we should judge frontier AI: **not by what one chatbot answers, but by what a *system* of thousands of agents can do** with enough compute and time.

**🔭 What to watch:** whether OpenAI releases the model behind this; independent verification of the proof; and whether the Buckmaster/Codex-logs claim produces receipts. TLDR also flagged a sharp counter-essay — that aiming industrial "solution-extraction" at math's best open problems **mines a non-renewable resource**, solving today's puzzles while starving tomorrow's progress.

Potentially a big accelerant for disease research. *[TLDR, The Neuron]*
